Data: Alpaca daily bars (feed `sip`, split/dividend adjusted), 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-20; CBOE VIX/VIX3M/VIX9D closes. Generated by `charts/signal_study.py`.

Columns: `n` signal days, `ep` distinct episodes (signal onsets), `mean`/`med` forward return, `hit` share of positive forward returns, `excess` = mean on signal days minus mean on other days, `t_nw` Newey-West t-stat of the excess (HAC lag = horizon; baseline row: t-stat of the mean), `fwdRV` mean forward 21d realized vol of that ticker (ann. %), `RVx` ratio vs non-signal days. `*` = fewer than 30 episodes (treat as anecdotal). |t| >= 2 bolded.

Caveat: overlapping horizons and 9 highly correlated tickers mean many tests are run on ~10 years of one market regime; expect a few |t|>2 by chance. Consistency across tickers matters more than any single t-stat.
