  • Risk sizing (approved):
    • 5% per name (SIZE_PCT_PER_NAME=5).
    • A 35% DAY buy-stop at the broker on every short (BROKER_STOP_PCT=35).
    • A 5% maximum squeeze-loss budget per name.
  • New gates:
    • At most 2 picks from the same TradingView industry.
    • No names with earnings within 2 sessions, using the events table plus Nasdaq. If Nasdaq can't be reached, the gate fails closed and drops the name.
